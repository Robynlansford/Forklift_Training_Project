# Module 2: Fork Slot Anatomy & Precision Alignment

**Core Philosophy:** Concert gear is expensive and delicate. One inch off with your forks can destroy $50k+ of LED panels or automation gear.

### [SCENARIO: SKIN VS SLOT]
You are approaching a custom scenic video wall cart in dim lighting. The bottom looks like solid aluminum skin.

**Question:** How do you guarantee your forks enter the hidden steel slots without spearing the cosmetic skin?

#### Correct Path
**Trainee:** Uses Vertical Rib Symmetry (align mast with top structural lines).

**AI Response:**  
"Brilliant. That is the **Vertical Rib Symmetry** technique. Even in darkness, the top welds and framing lines are perfectly aligned with the hidden slots below. Lining up your mast guarantees center entry."

### [SCENARIO: LEVEL FORK SHADOW TRICK]
You turn on your headlights. Shadows appear under your forks.

**Question:** How do you use the shadows to confirm your forks are level?

#### Correct Path
**Trainee:** Adjusts tilt until shadows are straight and parallel.

**AI Response:**  
"Perfect. That is the **Level-Fork Shadow Trick**. Straight, uniform shadows mean your blades are level — preventing gouging the bottom or puncturing the top of the pocket."

### [SCENARIO: TOP-HEAVY TIPPING]
The cart is top-heavy with motors at the top. You need to lift it using only one side temporarily.

**Question:** What risk do you recognize?

#### Correct Path
**Trainee:** Recognizes off-center lift will cause tipping and refuses single-side lift.

**AI Response:**  
"Correct. Top-heavy concert gear requires a wide, balanced stability triangle. Single-side lifting moves the center of gravity outside your base and will cause the cart to twist and crash."

**Tour Reality:** Precision fork work protects both the gear and the crew. One bad alignment can destroy a show piece or injure someone.
